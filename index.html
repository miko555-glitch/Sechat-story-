Core Narrative & Curse: On the ancient Giza Plateau, royal surveyor Henu falsified a temple-estate boundary stone. For every feddan of land that dies because of the stolen measure, one member of the marked bloodline dies at age twenty-one: Saleh (1986), Ehab (1995), Samir (2006), Hossam (2015), Adam (2026), and Tarek (2045)—whose initials spell S-E-S-H-A-T.

Seshat X Scientific Guardrails:

  • Input Layer: Sentinel-2 optical, NASA Terra MODIS thermal/ET, NASA SMAP regional soil moisture, Landsat 8/9 thermal, NASA POWER & ECMWF ERA5-Land meteorology, local soil lab EC/pH, crop calendars, and irrigation telemetry.
  • Analytics Layer: FAO-56 Penman-Monteith ($ET_0$, $ET_c$, Dual Crop Coefficient), Temperature-Vegetation Dryness Index (TVDI), CatBoost crop-health classification, and NDVI spatial coefficient of variation ($CV$) for mechanical anomaly flagging.
  • Strict Scientific Honesty: Synthetic demonstration values are never presented as raw satellite observations; SMAP regional spatial resolution is never treated as single-field point data; satellite NDVI variation flags potential center-pivot issues for physical inspection rather than confirming a blocked nozzle; and NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 (2041–2060) projections are treated as conditional scenarios rather than guaranteed forecasts.
